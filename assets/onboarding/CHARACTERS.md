# Coupon Master onboarding characters

## Blue investigator

- Role: curious guide who checks coupon details.
- Prop: magnifying glass.
- Motion: lean in, small head tilt, calm vertical bob.
- Appears during image/text recognition and validation.

## Mint helper

- Role: friendly assistant who points to the next action and celebrates progress.
- Motion: point, short hop, small side tilt.
- Appears during field extraction, preview, save, and success.

## Motion rules

- Idle loops stay subtle and under 10 px of travel.
- User-triggered reactions finish within 700 ms.
- Loading motion may loop; celebrations play once.
- Never animate more than two characters plus one functional object at once.
- Reduced-motion mode shows the final pose without looping.
- Blue remains `#2864F0`; mint remains `#58DFC6`.
- Characters stay gender-neutral and never use speech bubbles for essential instructions.
